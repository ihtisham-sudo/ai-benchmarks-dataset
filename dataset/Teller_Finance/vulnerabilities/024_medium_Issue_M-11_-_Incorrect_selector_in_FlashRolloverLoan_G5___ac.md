VulnerabilityDetail FlashRolloverLoan_G5: : _acceptCommitment () accepts the commitment to SmartCommitmentForwarder if _commitmentArgs.smartCommitmentAddress ! = address (0) . However, the selector used is acceptSmartCommitmentWithRecipient () , which does notmatch SmartCommitmentForwarder: : acceptCommitmentWithRecipient () ,DoSing the ability to rollover loans for LenderCommitmentGroup_Smart. Impact FlashRolloverLoan_G5will notwork for LenderCommitmentGroup_Smart loans.
